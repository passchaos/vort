# Rejected x86-64 Zstd large-JSON follow-ups

This 2026-10-01 investigation re-profiled the synthetic 512 KiB `large_json`
row after the FSE/RLE/RLE pair work. Vort reaches only 0.8045x median / 0.8064x
geometric local libzstd 1.6.0-dev throughput, so this remains a real decode
gap rather than a control-only workload.

The frame contains four full all-FSE/four-stream-Huffman blocks plus one tiny
tail block. The four selected blocks contain 7,298 sequences, 8,329 literals,
and 515,959 match bytes. A frozen prepared interleaved stage takes about
77.5 microseconds per frame while complete decode is roughly 84 microseconds;
about 92% of current time is therefore inside literal expansion plus the
sequence/copy executor, not frame parsing or FSE/Huffman table setup.

## Measured sequence structure

The existing compact pair path sees 663 two-sequence pairs in the locked
128 KiB oracle. The successor after a pair is another pair 563 times, a direct
offset sequence 78 times, and a 2--4-byte rep0 sequence 12 times. Four sequences
cannot share the current 56-bit field group: a direct four-sequence attempt
had zero hits. Match-length pairs are even more concentrated:

- `68/68`: 4 pairs;
- `69/69`: 27 pairs;
- `70/70`: 336 pairs;
- `71/71`: 291 pairs;
- mixed 68--71 lengths: 5 pairs.

The Huffman blocks have maximum widths `10/7/7/7`. The first block regenerates
2,083 literals from a 977-byte compressed literal section; the remaining
width-seven blocks already use the eight-symbol reservoir path.

## Rejected executor and copy variants

All ratios below are candidate over the frozen `cc519d45` implementation.
Every candidate preserved the prepared-stage checksum and passed either the
locked large-JSON specialized-versus-generic oracle or the complete-frame
byte-for-byte output check before timing.

- A separate noinline consecutive-pair executor expanded to 2.7 KiB and
  measured 0.7736x median / 0.7728x geometric across 15 stage pairs, 0/15 wins.
- Turning the existing inline pair block into an internal run loop measured
  0.9552x / 0.9535x, 0/15. Its loop-carried dependency cost exceeded the saved
  outer dispatch.
- Fusing the 99.2% same-length 68--71 pair branch measured 0.9918x median with
  4/15 wins. One 1.80x host outlier made its geometric result misleadingly
  positive; the median and ordinary observations were negative.
- Keeping LL/ML/OF FSE states in local scalars expanded the two relevant
  instances by roughly 0.8--1.1 KiB and measured 0.9640x median / 0.9620x
  geometric with 0/7 wins.
- A 2026-10-02 follow-up preloaded the current LL and OF FSE entries across the
  pair body. Although the target executor shrank from 8,822 to 8,632 bytes,
  the longer live ranges reduced complete-frame throughput to 0.9848x median /
  0.9866x geometric with 1/7 wins. The candidate was removed; the succeeding
  rep0-range fusion is documented separately in
  [zstd-pair-rep0-range-x86.md](zstd-pair-rep0-range-x86.md).
- Reordering the existing 70-byte copy branch ahead of the 71-byte branch lost
  all seven prepared-stage and complete-frame pairs, at 0.9758x and 0.9889x
  geometric throughput. Replacing both exact 70-byte tails with one overlapping
  eight-byte tail was also neutral-negative at 0.9966x stage / 0.9996x frame.
  Both candidates were removed. The retained first-match-only wild tail is
  documented in
  [zstd-pair-70-wild-tail-x86.md](zstd-pair-70-wild-tail-x86.md).
- Extending that wild-tail rule to the 71-byte pair head improved the prepared
  stage but regressed complete frames to 0.9973x geometric throughput. Applying
  it to a pair's second 70-byte match when another sequence remained was only
  1.0011x at the frame median; one 1.1510x scheduler outlier hid an otherwise
  neutral 1.0001x libzstd-normalized geometric result. Both were removed.
- Restricting a local output cursor to the two-sequence pair, rather than the
  complete executor, still regressed the prepared stage to 0.9858x and the
  frame to 0.9809x geometric throughput with 0/7 wins. A parameter-level
  `noalias` proof for `written` generated byte-identical binaries, confirming
  that LLVM already understood that alias relationship. Both were removed.
- Compiling the compact split executor without its three prefixed pair,
  short-rep0, and direct-offset paths, while retaining the established
  long-match wildcopy policy, shrank the production instance from 9,833 to
  6,116 bytes and the complete split section from 18,678 to 11,329 bytes. Five
  prepared-stage pairs nevertheless measured only 0.8234x median / 0.8223x
  geometric throughput with 0/5 wins.
- Also disabling the compact long-match wildcopy policy reduced the production
  instance further to 5,201 bytes and the section to 10,433 bytes, but seven
  prepared-stage pairs fell to 0.8078x median / 0.8073x geometric, 0/7. Both
  candidates preserved the prepared-stage checksum and were removed. The
  prefixed paths are a net performance requirement, not removable i-cache
  ballast.
- Restricting the two-sequence fast path up front to its measured 68--71-byte,
  non-overlapping match set removed the per-match range/overlap tests and cold
  fallback from the accepted pair body. The target instance shrank by 28 bytes,
  but seven fixed-CPU-2 complete-frame pairs regressed to 0.9295x median /
  0.9297x geometric raw throughput, with 0/7 wins; simultaneous libzstd
  normalization was 0.9259x / 0.9283x. The existing late per-match dispatch is
  therefore materially better despite its larger static body.
- Issuing an x86 read prefetch for the validated pair match source added only
  16 bytes to the target executor, but seven complete-frame pairs measured
  0.9943x median / 0.9944x geometric throughput with 1/7 wins. Preloading both
  pair literals and advancing their cursor once reduced the executor by four
  bytes, but measured 0.9921x / 0.9914x with 0/7 wins. Both were removed; the
  pair's current load timing and literal cursor remain preferable.
- Sending the final two sequences through the ordinary loop removed the pair
  path's terminal-state branch but measured 0.9947x median / 0.9945x geometric
  throughput with 0/7 wins. Combining each LL/OF shape pair into one masked
  predicate shrank the target instance by 168 bytes but regressed it to
  0.9704x / 0.9713x, also with 0/7 wins. Both were removed.
- Marking zero-width transition branches likely measured only 0.9829x median /
  0.9824x geometric target throughput with 0/7 wins. Applying branchless
  masked extraction to match-extra fields as well as transitions improved the
  target by 1.0291x geometrically, but was weaker than the transition-only
  form and moved three one-shot long controls negative. Both were removed; the
  retained transition-only result is documented separately in
  [zstd-pair-branchless-transitions-x86.md](zstd-pair-branchless-transitions-x86.md).
- An exact 64-byte forward loop for the separate `large_text` row's four
  distance-82, approximately 128 KiB overlapping matches measured only
  0.5681x / 0.5689x with 0/15 wins. The retained logarithmic seed-and-double
  copier is decisively better.

## Rejected Huffman and lookup variants

- A standalone six-symbol width-ten reservoir loop improved the prepared stage
  by 1.0075x median / 1.0091x geometric, 10/11, but grew the helper by about
  1.5 KiB.
- Folding the sixth symbol into the existing five-symbol loop reduced the
  growth to 178 bytes and kept a 1.0069x / 1.0085x stage gain with 14/15 wins.
  Fifteen complete-frame pairs, however, measured only 1.0006x median / 1.0030x
  geometric; simultaneous libzstd normalization was 0.9988x geometric. It was
  removed.
- Filling the complete 11-bit lookup 16 entries at a time instead of eight
  measured only 1.0029x stage geometric and shifted the following Huffman text
  section by 256 bytes. It was removed without a full-frame campaign.
- Extending X2 to the 2--4 KiB, 1,536--2,047-sequence low-literal band measured
  0.9943x stage median / 0.9984x geometric with 1/7 wins. It also exposed an
  X2 `CorruptFrame` versus canonical `TruncatedFrame` mismatch on a mutated
  stream; the selector and temporary cold fallback were both removed.

These results rule out another round of local branch growth, wider copy loops,
larger Huffman batches, table-fill tuning, or shrinking the executor by
removing its prefixed paths. The next credible `large_json` direction is a
genuinely smaller independently compiled executor/state representation that
preserves the current two-sequence, direct-offset, short-rep0, and long-copy
semantics without pointerized calls or duplicated error exits.

Exact binary hashes, commands, and summary ratios are recorded in
[zstd-large-json-followups-x86-20261001.json](data/zstd-large-json-followups-x86-20261001.json).

A later output-cursor experiment improved the target but regressed controls
when its incidental code placement changed. The resumed fixed-NOP padding,
a page-aligned variant, and a bounded-wildcopy-only local cursor were all
removed. The complete-row evidence and retained validation work are recorded
in [decoder-distance-cursor-x86.md](decoder-distance-cursor-x86.md).
