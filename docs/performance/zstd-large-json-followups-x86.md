# Rejected x86-64 Zstd large-JSON follow-ups

This 2026-10-01 investigation re-profiled the synthetic 512 KiB `large_json`
row after the FSE/RLE/RLE pair work. Vort reaches only 0.8045x median / 0.8064x
geometric local libzstd 1.6.0-dev throughput, so this remains a real decode
gap rather than a control-only workload.

The frame contains four full all-FSE/four-stream-Huffman blocks plus one tiny
tail block. The four selected blocks contain 7,298 sequences, 8,329 literals,
and 515,959 match bytes. A frozen prepared interleaved stage takes about
77.5 microseconds per frame while complete decode is roughly 84 microseconds;
about 92% of current time is therefore inside literal expansion plus the
sequence/copy executor, not frame parsing or FSE/Huffman table setup.

## Measured sequence structure

The existing compact pair path sees 663 two-sequence pairs in the locked
128 KiB oracle. The successor after a pair is another pair 563 times, a direct
offset sequence 78 times, and a 2--4-byte rep0 sequence 12 times. Four sequences
cannot share the current 56-bit field group: a direct four-sequence attempt
had zero hits. Match-length pairs are even more concentrated:

- `68/68`: 4 pairs;
- `69/69`: 27 pairs;
- `70/70`: 336 pairs;
- `71/71`: 291 pairs;
- mixed 68--71 lengths: 5 pairs.

The Huffman blocks have maximum widths `10/7/7/7`. The first block regenerates
2,083 literals from a 977-byte compressed literal section; the remaining
width-seven blocks already use the eight-symbol reservoir path.

## Rejected executor and copy variants

All ratios below are candidate over the frozen `cc519d45` implementation.
Every candidate preserved the prepared-stage checksum and passed either the
locked large-JSON specialized-versus-generic oracle or the complete-frame
byte-for-byte output check before timing.

- A separate noinline consecutive-pair executor expanded to 2.7 KiB and
  measured 0.7736x median / 0.7728x geometric across 15 stage pairs, 0/15 wins.
- Turning the existing inline pair block into an internal run loop measured
  0.9552x / 0.9535x, 0/15. Its loop-carried dependency cost exceeded the saved
  outer dispatch.
- Fusing the 99.2% same-length 68--71 pair branch measured 0.9918x median with
  4/15 wins. One 1.80x host outlier made its geometric result misleadingly
  positive; the median and ordinary observations were negative.
- Keeping LL/ML/OF FSE states in local scalars expanded the two relevant
  instances by roughly 0.8--1.1 KiB and measured 0.9640x median / 0.9620x
  geometric with 0/7 wins.
- A 2026-10-02 follow-up preloaded the current LL and OF FSE entries across the
  pair body. Although the target executor shrank from 8,822 to 8,632 bytes,
  the longer live ranges reduced complete-frame throughput to 0.9848x median /
  0.9866x geometric with 1/7 wins. The candidate was removed; the succeeding
  rep0-range fusion is documented separately in
  [zstd-pair-rep0-range-x86.md](zstd-pair-rep0-range-x86.md).
- Reordering the existing 70-byte copy branch ahead of the 71-byte branch lost
  all seven prepared-stage and complete-frame pairs, at 0.9758x and 0.9889x
  geometric throughput. Replacing both exact 70-byte tails with one overlapping
  eight-byte tail was also neutral-negative at 0.9966x stage / 0.9996x frame.
  Both candidates were removed. The retained first-match-only wild tail is
  documented in
  [zstd-pair-70-wild-tail-x86.md](zstd-pair-70-wild-tail-x86.md).
- Extending that wild-tail rule to the 71-byte pair head improved the prepared
  stage but regressed complete frames to 0.9973x geometric throughput. Applying
  it to a pair's second 70-byte match when another sequence remained was only
  1.0011x at the frame median; one 1.1510x scheduler outlier hid an otherwise
  neutral 1.0001x libzstd-normalized geometric result. Both were removed.
- Restricting a local output cursor to the two-sequence pair, rather than the
  complete executor, still regressed the prepared stage to 0.9858x and the
  frame to 0.9809x geometric throughput with 0/7 wins. A parameter-level
  `noalias` proof for `written` generated byte-identical binaries, confirming
  that LLVM already understood that alias relationship. Both were removed.
- Compiling the compact split executor without its three prefixed pair,
  short-rep0, and direct-offset paths, while retaining the established
  long-match wildcopy policy, shrank the production instance from 9,833 to
  6,116 bytes and the complete split section from 18,678 to 11,329 bytes. Five
  prepared-stage pairs nevertheless measured only 0.8234x median / 0.8223x
  geometric throughput with 0/5 wins.
- Also disabling the compact long-match wildcopy policy reduced the production
  instance further to 5,201 bytes and the section to 10,433 bytes, but seven
  prepared-stage pairs fell to 0.8078x median / 0.8073x geometric, 0/7. Both
  candidates preserved the prepared-stage checksum and were removed. The
  prefixed paths are a net performance requirement, not removable i-cache
  ballast.
- Restricting the two-sequence fast path up front to its measured 68--71-byte,
  non-overlapping match set removed the per-match range/overlap tests and cold
  fallback from the accepted pair body. The target instance shrank by 28 bytes,
  but seven fixed-CPU-2 complete-frame pairs regressed to 0.9295x median /
  0.9297x geometric raw throughput, with 0/7 wins; simultaneous libzstd
  normalization was 0.9259x / 0.9283x. The existing late per-match dispatch is
  therefore materially better despite its larger static body.
- Issuing an x86 read prefetch for the validated pair match source added only
  16 bytes to the target executor, but seven complete-frame pairs measured
  0.9943x median / 0.9944x geometric throughput with 1/7 wins. Preloading both
  pair literals and advancing their cursor once reduced the executor by four
  bytes, but measured 0.9921x / 0.9914x with 0/7 wins. Both were removed; the
  pair's current load timing and literal cursor remain preferable.
- Sending the final two sequences through the ordinary loop removed the pair
  path's terminal-state branch but measured 0.9947x median / 0.9945x geometric
  throughput with 0/7 wins. Combining each LL/OF shape pair into one masked
  predicate shrank the target instance by 168 bytes but regressed it to
  0.9704x / 0.9713x, also with 0/7 wins. Both were removed.
- Marking zero-width transition branches likely measured only 0.9829x median /
  0.9824x geometric target throughput with 0/7 wins. Applying branchless
  masked extraction to match-extra fields as well as transitions improved the
  target by 1.0291x geometrically, but was weaker than the transition-only
  form and moved three one-shot long controls negative. Both were removed; the
  retained transition-only result is documented separately in
  [zstd-pair-branchless-transitions-x86.md](zstd-pair-branchless-transitions-x86.md).
- Branching once around each complete zero-width transition triplet measured
  only 0.9723x median / 0.9699x geometric despite 57%/62% hit rates. Extracting
  each triplet in parallel and shifting the reservoir once measured 0.9931x /
  0.9928x. Requiring the target's observed nonzero match-extra widths measured
  0.9997x / 0.9996x, while a shifted OF control-word spelling generated
  identical target assembly. All four were removed. The succeeding independent
  pair-entry guards are documented in
  [zstd-pair-entry-guards-x86.md](zstd-pair-entry-guards-x86.md).
- Narrowing all three block-level FSE states from `usize` to `u16` reduced the
  raw/all-FSE section but regressed `large_json` to 0.9757x median / 0.9784x
  geometric with 0/7 wins. Restricting 32-bit arithmetic to the pair's
  branchless masks was worse at a 0.9622x ordinary median; limiting it to the
  first next-state triplet was neutral-negative at 0.9992x / 0.9986x. All
  state-width candidates were removed.
- Localizing the compact executor's three repeated offsets and committing them
  with `defer` preserved partial-progress state on every error exit, but made
  LLVM duplicate that commit across the large function. The production
  instance grew from `0x257e` to `0x4109` bytes and the complete split section
  from `0x47bc` to `0x78f5`. Three clean complete-frame pairs regressed to
  0.7805x median / 0.7805x geometric, 0/3, and 0.7810x after libzstd
  normalization. Repeated offsets therefore remain in the caller-owned state.
- Reordering the internal `SequenceDecodeState` fields from LL/OF/ML to
  LL/ML/OF made the complete split section 19 bytes smaller and the strict
  instance 275 bytes smaller, but enlarged the production wildcopy instance
  from `0x257e` to `0x267e`. Three complete-frame pairs under the corrected
  continuous monitor all lost: 0.9787x median / 0.9769x geometric, and 0.9746x
  after libzstd normalization. The original field order was restored; a
  smaller aggregate section is not useful when its selected instance grows.
- Precomputing the match-FSE entry-array base for x86-64 ReleaseFast changed
  the three hot ML loads from `spec+0x2028+state*8` to `base+state*8`. It added
  no branch and shrank the production executor by 99 bytes and the split
  section by 100 bytes. The extra long-lived base register was nevertheless
  more expensive than the shorter addressing: seven clean complete-frame
  pairs measured 0.9874x median / 0.9876x geometric, 0/7, and 0.9866x after
  libzstd normalization. Direct table-base caching was removed; the offset
  table was not repeated because it has the same register-pressure tradeoff.
  Restricting the source-level match-table base to the successful pair scope
  did not shorten its generated live range: LLVM emitted a byte-identical
  `0x4758`-byte split-executor section for the pair-scoped and whole-function
  candidates, with SHA-256
  `fcf5cee3d344d67957a0235475c22b89c6db990e612482a2335eee69a72a8102`.
  The strict and production instances also retained the same `0x223d` and
  `0x251b` sizes. The identical machine code makes the existing clean 0/7
  result directly applicable, so timing was not repeated and the pair-scoped
  source candidate was removed.
- Three additional code-generation screens did not justify timing. Splitting
  each packed LL/OF shape mask into explicit base and additional-width tests
  produced a byte-identical `0x47bc` split section (SHA-256
  `2afa094dcee74d401b4069e3b270a954ca966b81387c3cb5d14d5561a33ec75d`).
  Making the complete generic split template noinline grew the production body
  from `0x257e` to `0x25de` and scattered four instances into ordinary text.
  Outlining only the pair's rare 68/69-byte copies reduced the aggregate split
  section to `0x46ed`, but call-site saves grew the production instance to
  `0x260a`. Marking the second LL/OF shape failure unlikely was also rejected
  before timing: despite only 45 failures after 708 first-shape hits, it grew
  the production instance to `0x28a1` and the split section to `0x49df`. All
  four source variants were removed.
- The first LL/OF pair shape succeeds only 708 times across the locked frame's
  6,636 outer iterations. Marking its failure edge likely shrank the strict
  instance from `0x223e` to `0x2144`, the production instance from `0x257e` to
  `0x2566`, and the split section from `0x47bc` to `0x46aa`. That smaller layout
  was substantially slower: seven clean complete-frame pairs measured 0.9475x
  median / 0.9480x geometric throughput with 0/7 wins, and 0.9452x after
  libzstd normalization. Pair success needs to remain the fallthrough despite
  its lower outer-loop frequency; the hint was removed.
- A structurally smaller production executor kept pair, direct-offset, and
  short-rep0 handling inline, but moved the remaining ordinary sequence into a
  noinline helper. Those prefixes cover 1,682/1,834 sequences in the locked
  block, so only 152 sequences (8.3%) crossed the new boundary. The production
  hot instance fell from `0x257e` to `0x10b6` while the strict instance stayed
  `0x223e`; a `0x1569` fallback moved into the later generic-split section.
  Nevertheless, three clean complete-frame pairs measured only 0.9522x median
  / 0.9521x geometric throughput with 0/3 wins, and 0.9478x after libzstd
  normalization. A separate seven-pair run was discarded because the corrected
  monitor caught an external Zig build. Even the lower-frequency pointerized
  call/state boundary is too expensive, so the candidate was removed.
- A copy-reuse audit found 658/663 pairs with equal match lengths, but only
  4/663 where the second match source exactly reused the first match output;
  neither dominant 70/70 nor 71/71 pair had that distance. A fused reload-free
  pair copier therefore has no useful coverage on the locked fixture.
- A pair-only literal-plus-71-byte copier packed the literal and first seven
  match bytes into one eight-byte store, followed by two 32-byte stores. The
  direct oracle covered distances 71--75 and all relevant destination
  alignments, but inlining the extra path grew the production pair executor
  from `0x257e` to `0x279a` bytes. Three complete-frame pairs under a corrected
  continuous process monitor regressed to 0.8574x median / 0.8578x geometric,
  0/3, and 0.8468x after libzstd normalization. At the minimum distance the
  required literal store precedes a partially overlapping 32-byte source load;
  that dependency plus the larger branch body overwhelms the saved store. The
  candidate was removed.
- An exact 64-byte forward loop for the separate `large_text` row's four
  distance-82, approximately 128 KiB overlapping matches measured only
  0.5681x / 0.5689x with 0/15 wins. The retained logarithmic seed-and-double
  copier is decisively better.

## Rejected Huffman and lookup variants

- A standalone six-symbol width-ten reservoir loop improved the prepared stage
  by 1.0075x median / 1.0091x geometric, 10/11, but grew the helper by about
  1.5 KiB.
- Folding the sixth symbol into the existing five-symbol loop reduced the
  growth to 178 bytes and kept a 1.0069x / 1.0085x stage gain with 14/15 wins.
  Fifteen complete-frame pairs, however, measured only 1.0006x median / 1.0030x
  geometric; simultaneous libzstd normalization was 0.9988x geometric. It was
  removed.
- A 2026-10-03 follow-up isolated the width-ten six-symbol loop in an exported
  `0xbd7`-byte section placed after every existing Zstd hot section. A cold
  call fit entirely inside the Huffman helper's existing alignment gap: the
  `.vort_zstd_huffman_only_decode` and compact-pair sections retained their
  addresses, sizes, and pair-instance addresses. A speculative five-symbol
  prefix plus loaded-word check safely increased the locked oracle's coverage
  from one to 81 six-symbol batches, about 1,944 literals. Despite that
  isolation and coverage, seven complete-frame pairs measured only 1.0004x
  median / 1.0004x geometric, 4/7, and 0.9960x after simultaneous libzstd
  normalization. Fifteen prepared-stage pairs measured 1.0003x median /
  1.0018x geometric, 9/15, with a 0.9896x--1.0303x range. The call/state
  round-trip consumed the local loop gain, so the helper and loaded-prefix
  API were removed.
- Batching the existing five-symbol loop's twenty scattered byte stores into
  four local five-byte records passed the locked specialized/generic oracle.
  LLVM emitted wider stores, but the reservoir helper grew from `0xb67` to
  `0xc51` bytes and shifted the unchanged compact-pair section by 256 bytes.
  Seven complete-frame pairs measured 1.0026x median / 1.0047x geometric,
  5/7; a single 1.0257x observation inflated the geometric result, while the
  libzstd-normalized median was only 1.0019x. That evidence did not justify
  the code growth and pair relocation, so direct interleaved byte stores were
  restored.
- Filling the complete 11-bit lookup 16 entries at a time instead of eight
  measured only 1.0029x stage geometric and shifted the following Huffman text
  section by 256 bytes. It was removed without a full-frame campaign.
- Extending X2 to the 2--4 KiB, 1,536--2,047-sequence low-literal band measured
  0.9943x stage median / 0.9984x geometric with 1/7 wins. It also exposed an
  X2 `CorruptFrame` versus canonical `TruncatedFrame` mismatch on a mutated
  stream; the selector and temporary cold fallback were both removed.
- A 2026-10-06 compact-state follow-up replaced the selected executor's
  88-byte `SequenceDecodeState` with a 64-byte representation that packed
  LL/OF/ML and remaining-sequence state into four `u16` lanes. Unlike the
  earlier global `u16` field experiment, this state was private to the compact
  executor and combined each three-state transition into one packed store.
  Extraction and reassembly still enlarged the complete-frame production
  instance from `0x257e` to `0x26c5` bytes and the split section from `0x47bc`
  to `0x4934`; the independently built prepared-stage instance grew from
  `0x257c` to `0x26b4`. Seven continuously monitored fixed-CPU-2 pairs
  regressed the prepared stage to 0.9297x median / 0.9268x geometric and the
  complete frame to 0.9426x / 0.9425x, both with 0/7 wins. All stage and frame
  checksums matched. The candidate was removed; exact samples and binary hashes
  are recorded in
  [zstd-packed-compact-state-x86-20261006.json](data/zstd-packed-compact-state-x86-20261006.json).
- A second 2026-10-06 follow-up kept the native-width 88-byte state but removed
  `frame_state` and `continuation` from the compact executor's per-block ABI.
  Successful callers committed repeated offsets after return; false and error
  paths retained the former no-commit semantics. The complete-frame production
  instance shrank from `0x257e` to `0x2550`, its strict sibling from `0x223e`
  to `0x2103`, and the split section from `0x47bc` to `0x4653`. The prepared
  binary similarly reduced its production instance from `0x257c` to `0x2553`
  and section from `0x47be` to `0x4643`. Seven clean prepared-stage pairs had
  a 1.0033x median, but one isolated 1.1625x slow baseline made the geometric
  result unusable; excluding it leaves only 1.0032x median / 1.0024x geometric.
  Three independent clean seven-pair complete-frame campaigns combine to
  0.9983x median / 0.9986x geometric throughput with 9/21 wins. Candidate
  libzstd-normalized geometry also fell from 0.8897x to 0.8870x. The candidate
  was removed despite its smaller executor because it did not improve the
  real target. Exact hashes and samples are in
  [zstd-compact-abi-followup-x86-20261006.json](data/zstd-compact-abi-followup-x86-20261006.json).
- A 2026-10-07 bitstream-model follow-up preloaded a 16-byte reverse-bit window
  for one successful pair and derived the next pair's 64-bit shifting group
  from its lower word. The cached pair avoided a second reader position
  calculation and memory load; the locked oracle exercised this route more
  than 100 times and randomized ReleaseFast/ReleaseSafe tests covered the
  two-word handoff. The extra cached state and live 128-bit preload enlarged
  the production instance from `0x2530` to `0x25c1`, the strict instance from
  `0x220e` to `0x22f6`, and the split section from `0x473e` to `0x48b7`.
  Seven clean fixed-layout complete-frame pairs regressed to `0.9390x` median /
  `0.9387x` geometric throughput with 0/7 wins. The source was restored;
  details are in
  [zstd-pair-window-x86-20261007.json](data/zstd-pair-window-x86-20261007.json).
- A later 2026-10-07 FSE-builder follow-up reused the existing four-entry
  packed writer for the two dominant-symbol runs in the 255/256 one-gap path.
  The fixed and randomized 256-state table oracle passed, and the specialized
  builder plus `.vort_zstd_fse_256` section shrank by 127 bytes (`0x1188` to
  `0x1109`) without moving the preceding Huffman or sequence executors. A
  clean seven-pair, fixed-CPU-2 `large_json` campaign nevertheless measured
  only `0.9969x` median / `0.9976x` geometric raw throughput with 2/7 wins.
  Same-process libzstd 1.6.0 normalization was neutral-negative at `0.9972x`
  median / `0.9990x` geometric with 3/7 wins. The scalar one-gap loops were
  restored; their compiler lowering is faster than the explicit packed-run
  setup despite the larger static builder.

These results rule out another round of local branch growth, wider copy loops,
larger Huffman batches, table-fill tuning, or shrinking the executor by
removing its prefixed paths. They now also rule out integer-lane packing as a
smaller state representation: the hot extraction/reassembly cost dominates
the 24-byte call-boundary reduction. Moving the one-time continuation commit
out of the executor does make its section smaller, but does not improve the
complete frame either. The next credible `large_json` direction is therefore
not another local state/ABI spelling. A wider preload that merely caches the
next pair is also insufficient: future table/bitstream work must remove more
than one location/load without extending hot state or live ranges, while
preserving the current two-sequence, direct-offset, short-rep0, and long-copy
semantics. Smaller FSE-builder code is likewise insufficient when it adds
packed-run setup to the 255/256 distribution.
